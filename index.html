About SpongeBob

SpongeBob SquarePants is an endlessly optimistic sea sponge who resides in a pineapple under the sea in Bikini Bottom. He spends his days cooking Krabby Patties, practicing karate, and catching jellyfish in Jellyfish Fields.

Friends & Neighbors

Patrick Star

SpongeBob's best friend, a pink starfish who lives under a rock and enjoys jellyfishing and sleeping.

Squidward Tentacles

SpongeBob's next-door neighbor and cashier coworker at the Krusty Krab, who plays the clarinet and wants to be famous.

The Krusty Krab

Owned by Eugene H. Krabs, the Krusty Krab is Bikini Bottom's premier eatery, famous for its legendary sandwich.
